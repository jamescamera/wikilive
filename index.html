Off air Catch mode
HEARING —
Nothing caught yet Press START, point your mic at the telly, then hit CATCH THAT when something goes past you and you want to know what it was.

Or turn on AMBIENT and it'll surface things on its own while you watch.

Needs a mic, Chrome, and an API key — add one under SETUP.

The bar next to the status lamp is your input level. If it moves when you talk, the mic is working.

SETUP

Your key is stored in this browser only and sent straight to Anthropic. Nothing is stored on the site, so this is safe to host publicly — every visitor uses their own key.

Audio never leaves your machine. Chrome turns speech into text locally; only that text is sent.
